## Long description (Amazon limit: 4,000 characters, plain text)

Learn Anything. Solve Everything.

EduMind AI is a free AI study companion for school, college and university students, teachers and independent learners. Ask a question in your own words and get a clear, step-by-step answer in plain language — no confusing symbols, no copied answers, just help you can actually use.

Ask AI, get answers that make sense
Type or speak your question and get a structured answer: a direct answer first, then a simple explanation, step-by-step working, formulas where they matter, examples, and the key points to remember. Every answer is written in plain text, so fractions look like 1/2 and powers look like x^2 — nothing strange on screen. Copy, share, regenerate or save any answer, and keep your chat history to revise later.

16 subjects, one app
Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, History, Geography, Computer Science, Economics, Psychology, Art and Design, Music, Languages, Business, Medicine and Law. Every subject is covered from school level to university level, so the same app grows with you.

20 AI study tools
Quiz Generator, Flashcards, Document Summarizer, Essay Helper, Formula Solver, Camera Scanner and more. Turn any topic into a quiz, memorise key facts with flashcards, summarise long notes in seconds, and plan better essays.

Scan it or upload it
Stuck on a printed question? Point your camera at it. Have a PDF, Word document or slides? Upload it and ask questions about it, or get a summary with the main ideas and key points. Documents are processed in memory only and are never stored on our servers.

Made for real studying
Set daily goals, build a streak, and earn XP as you learn. Switch between light, dark and system themes. No account is needed — start learning as a guest right away.

Free to use
EduMind AI is free and supported by ads. Ads appear only at natural breaks, never while you are reading an answer, scanning, or uploading a document.

Download EduMind AI today and make every study session count.
