Privacy Policy — EduMind AI

Effective date: November 11, 2026  •  Last updated: November 11, 2026

EduMind AI ("the App") is a free AI study app for students, teachers and independent learners. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Questions you ask. When you ask the AI a question, your question is sent to our secure backend server and to Google's Gemini AI service so an answer can be generated. We do not attach your name, email address or phone number to your questions.

Documents and photos you upload. If you upload a document (PDF, Word, text or presentation file) or scan a page with your camera, the file is processed in memory only so the App can read it and answer questions about it. Uploaded files are not stored on our servers and are discarded after processing.

Chat history and preferences. Your past questions, saved answers, daily goals, streaks and settings (such as light or dark theme) are stored only on your own device. We do not keep a copy on our servers.

Advertising identifiers. The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and general, non-personal information (such as device type and approximate location) to show and measure ads. We do not receive your name or contact details from AdMob.

No account is required. You can use the App as a guest. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or home address inside the App.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate AI answers, summaries, quizzes and flashcards from your questions and uploads.
  • To show your own chat history, saved answers and learning progress on your device.
  • To display ads that keep the App free, through Google AdMob.
  • To fix problems and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use your questions or documents for advertising.

3. Third-party services

The App uses these third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini AI — generates the AI answers. Questions you ask are processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob — shows ads in the App. AdMob's data practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob help pages.

4. Data retention

Uploaded documents and scanned images are held in memory only while they are being processed, then discarded. Questions sent to Gemini are handled under Google's policies. Chat history and settings stay on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App.

5. Children's privacy

EduMind AI is designed for students aged 13 and older. If you are under 13, please use the App only with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not require any child to provide personal information to use the App.

6. Your choices

  • Delete your chat history and saved answers at any time inside the App.
  • Turn off personalised ads in your device settings (Google Settings > Ads, or your device's privacy settings).
  • Uninstall the App at any time — this removes the data stored on your device.

7. Data security

Questions are sent to our backend over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and your Gemini API key is kept only on our server — it is never included in the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, tell you inside the App.
